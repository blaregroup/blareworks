Services

What we specialize in

The work we’re built for — and the work we won’t fake.

Accent walls

Feature walls that change the room without pretending the rest of the house doesn’t matter. Clean lines, solid installs, finishes that hold up.

  • Slat, fluted, and media walls
  • Paint and trim that actually match
  • Detail work people notice

Baths & kitchens

The rooms that expose every shortcut. We rebuild them so the cabinets, tile, trim, and paint look like one decision — not five abandoned ones.

  • Full and partial remodels
  • Fixture and finish upgrades
  • Cleanup of half-done gut jobs

Commercial interiors

Offices, suites, and small business spaces that need a sharp interior refresh without a circus on site.

  • Tenant improvements
  • Finish packages
  • Takeover / incomplete build-outs

We do not do roofs. Ever. Additions aren’t on the menu yet.

Process

How a Blare job usually goes

Less “perfect Gantt chart.” More “what’s actually wrong, and how do we make it look finished.”

  1. Walk the mess

    We look at what’s incomplete, what got abandoned, and what still bothers you every time you walk past it.

  2. Name the real scope

    Not a fantasy list — the work that closes the gaps and makes the space feel done.

  3. Do the work

    Accent walls, baths, kitchens, commercial interiors — with the unfinished edges actually finished.

  4. Hand it back tight

    You shouldn’t still see raw boards, open trim, or “we’ll get to it later” staring at you.

About

Unfinished details aren’t details.

Unpainted boards and open trim don’t read as “in progress.” They read as unfinished everything else — a window into the house, and the life inside it. We take those eyesores seriously.

Blare is for the projects that need a closer: accent walls, baths, kitchens, commercial interiors, and the work other crews left hanging. We’re not pitching ourselves as the tightest schedule on earth. We’re the crew that makes it look finished.
